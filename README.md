# 📝 Notes App — Personal Life OS

*(Android Productivity Application — Project Overview)*

---

## 📱 Overview

**Notes App** is an Android productivity application designed to bring notes, tasks, ideas, shopping lists, and reminders together in one organized workspace.

Beyond basic note-taking, the vision is to build a **Personal Life OS for Notes** that helps users capture thoughts, organize information, manage everyday activities, and revisit important memories.

**Think → Capture → Organize → Act → Remember**

---

## 💡 What Makes Notes App Different

Traditional notes applications primarily focus on storing text. Notes App aims to connect **note-taking, personal productivity, and everyday-life organization** in a unified experience.

From study notes and task management to grocery checklists, scheduled reminders, and related ideas, the goal is to help users manage more of their daily lives from a single application.

---

## ⚙️ Key Capabilities

### 📝 Smart Notes & Organization

- Multiple note types: Quick Note, Task, Study Note, Idea, Checklist, Event, and Private Note
- Search by title, content, type, and future tags or categories
- Priorities, pinning, archiving, and organized note collections
- Related notes to connect projects, ideas, and personal goals
- Rich content support for text, images, files, audio, and links

### 🏠 Smart Dashboard

- Personalized greeting and profile information
- Quick Capture and convenient note creation
- Notes and task statistics
- Recent notes and search access
- Planned focus-oriented sections and upcoming reminders

### 🛒 Grocery & Shopping Lists

- Multiple lists with individual checklist items
- Add, edit, delete, and mark items as purchased
- Quantities and completed-item tracking
- Categories for groceries, household items, shopping, and errands
- Planned reusable list templates

### ⏰ Smart Reminders

- Attach reminders to notes and tasks
- Select dates and times
- Android notifications for scheduled reminders
- Upcoming, overdue, and completed reminder tracking
- Planned rescheduling and recurring reminders

### 🕒 Personal Timeline & Privacy

- Chronological access to notes and memories
- Browse past ideas and important moments
- Planned private-note classification, PIN protection, and biometric locking
- Future offline-first access and synchronization improvements

*These capabilities represent the product vision; not all features are currently implemented.*

---

## 🧠 Architecture Highlights

- User-specific data organized under Firebase Authentication UIDs
- Structured Cloud Firestore storage for profiles and notes
- Timestamp-based note history foundation
- Planned structured data models for checklists, shopping lists, reminders, and related notes
- Firebase Storage planned for media and file attachments
- Offline reliability, advanced privacy, and additional synchronization behaviour planned for future development
